About the System

Sit-in Monitoring System

A comprehensive platform for managing and tracking student sit-in sessions in the College of Information & Computer Studies laboratory at University of Cebu.

Version 1.0.0
Developed by University of Cebu
Purpose Track lab attendance

System Features

Student Dashboard

View your profile, session history, and remaining sessions.

Reservation System

Book your preferred laboratory schedule in advance.

Session Tracking

Monitor your sit-in hours and track laboratory usage.

Announcements

Stay updated with lab rules and important notices.

Profile Management

Update your personal information and contact details.

History Log

Access your complete sit-in history and statistics.

Developer Information

About the Developers

This system was developed by students of the College of Computer Studies (CCS) at University of Cebu as part of their academic project.

The system aims to automate and streamline the traditional paper-based method of tracking student sit-in sessions in computer laboratories.
